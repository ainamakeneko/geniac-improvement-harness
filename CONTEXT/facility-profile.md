# Facility Profile

This profile is intentionally de-identified and contains no service-user personal data.

## Observed context
- Small disability-welfare/work-support organization.
- Roughly 30–40 service users and about 10 staff across several sites.
- Activities include light assembly, vegetable/confectionery sales, cleaning, pottery, indigo dyeing, café work, and confectionery production.
- Administrative work includes individual support planning, daily records, government submissions, disaster-related documentation, inventory/communication tasks, and event preparation.
- Some workflows remain paper-heavy and depend strongly on experienced staff.
- Staff time and budget for dedicated DX work are limited.

## Important constraints
- Do not assume all listed activities occur at every site.
- Do not assume current software, paper forms, staffing patterns, or process ownership beyond what is explicitly observed.
- Real service-user data must not be added to this repository.
