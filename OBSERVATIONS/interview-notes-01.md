# Interview / Observation Notes 01

These are de-identified operational observations assembled for the first harness experiment. They are not a complete process map.

## Observed
- Daily record entry can take around one hour per day. The cause is not yet established; slow PCs or the workflow itself may contribute.
- Some administrative submissions require counting or aggregating numbers of service users, and staff describe this as burdensome.
- Individual support plans are created with staff interviews and data entry; at least some local spreadsheet-based support exists.
- Paper approval / stamped-document workflows remain substantial.
- Event-related work can become urgent, including applications, equipment procurement or rental decisions, and equipment preparation.
- Indigo dyeing involves a container of roughly 60 L, periodic stirring, and handwritten temperature/pH-related records. Weekend staffing constraints can make attendance for maintenance burdensome.
- Operations have historically relied on people manually absorbing exceptions and keeping work moving.
- Staff are busy and there is limited money and time available for improvement work.

## Unknown
- Which of these burdens consumes the most staff hours over a month or year.
- Which tasks cause the most errors, rework, delay, stress, or service-user impact.
- Exact current systems, file formats, process ownership, approval rules, and regulatory requirements for each workflow.
- Which tasks staff would most willingly test a change on.
- Whether the one-hour daily-record burden is primarily system latency, duplicate entry, navigation, policy requirements, staffing patterns, or another cause.
- Whether weekend indigo-dye stirring can safely be automated; no safety or process-control assessment has been completed.

## Reminder
Do not convert the unknowns above into facts. The first run should discover improvement seeds and propose a small evidence-gathering or improvement experiment, not attempt to redesign the organization.
