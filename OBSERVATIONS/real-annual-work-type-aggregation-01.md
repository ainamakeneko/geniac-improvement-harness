# Real observation — annual work-type aggregation for government submission

> REAL FACILITY OBSERVATION. This file contains only sanitized operational facts shared by the human operator. Do not add personal data or infer missing institutional facts.

## OBSERVED

- The human operator joined the work partway through rather than observing the process from the beginning.
- The task was to prepare source data for a document to be submitted to a government office for an application described by the operator as possibly related to a subsidy.
- The source information represented, for each service user, the type of work performed on each day.
- The task required aggregating approximately one year of records.
- Monthly data had existed in Excel form at some point.
- The original Excel files were not available to the operator during this work.
- The available source material was printed paper.
- Approximately twelve months of printed records were manually aggregated.
- The operator performed part of this aggregation work.

## HYPOTHESES TO TEST — NOT FACTS

- Preserving the monthly source Excel files may reduce future annual aggregation effort.
- A stable monthly data structure may permit a small annual aggregation workbook or script.
- If only paper is available, OCR or structured transcription could reduce manual counting, but privacy, accuracy, and correction burden may make it a poor choice.
- The largest opportunity may be preventing loss/unavailability of structured monthly data rather than accelerating paper counting.
- A small process or file-management change may outperform building a new application.

## Safety / privacy boundary

- Do not place service-user names, identifiers, diagnoses, support details, addresses, or other personal data in this repository.
- Do not upload unredacted source records to external AI services as part of this experiment.
- Do not change official records, required documentation, storage controls, or submission procedures without explicit human approval.
