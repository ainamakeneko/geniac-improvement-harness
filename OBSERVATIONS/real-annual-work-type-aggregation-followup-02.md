- The operator's expectation that the task occurs every year is current best evidence, but the exact formal recurrence and ownership have not yet been confirmed by the responsible process owner.
- The fact that monthly formats were almost the same suggests bounded structural variation, but the exact column/category compatibility has not been inspected.

## Decision relevance

The new evidence materially increases the plausibility of an upstream structured-data solution because the task likely recurs annually and monthly formats are broadly stable. However, the existence/access status of the original Excel files remains the key branching fact: if they still exist and can be retained safely, prevention/file-management or spreadsheet aggregation is likely preferable to optimizing paper/OCR fallback. If they do not exist or cannot be used, the paper fallback path must be evaluated separately with full verification and privacy costs included.
