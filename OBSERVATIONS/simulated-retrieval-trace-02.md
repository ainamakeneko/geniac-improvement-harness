# Simulated Retrieval Trace 02

> SIMULATED EVIDENCE FOR HARNESS TESTING ONLY. This is not real facility evidence.

This dataset represents a hypothetical result of the retrieval-trace measurement proposed in Experiment 2. It is deliberately more consistent than the earlier work sample so the harness must decide whether continued observation still has enough value to justify delaying a small intervention.

## Session 1

- Total daily-record preparation: 53 minutes
- Direct entry: 14 minutes
- Retrieving prior paper records: 9 minutes
- Retrieving files from shared storage: 7 minutes
- Checking / confirmation: 12 minutes
- PC/system waiting: 6 minutes
- Other / transition: 5 minutes
- The same small set of prior reference materials was used repeatedly during the session.
- No service-user content was recorded in this trace.

## Session 2

- Total daily-record preparation: 54 minutes
- Direct entry: 13 minutes
- Retrieving prior paper records: 11 minutes
- Retrieving files from shared storage: 8 minutes
- Checking / confirmation: 10 minutes
- PC/system waiting: 7 minutes
- Other / transition: 5 minutes
- Most retrieved material belonged to the same categories as Session 1.
- No service-user content was recorded in this trace.

## Session 3

- Total daily-record preparation: 56 minutes
- Direct entry: 15 minutes
- Retrieving prior paper records: 10 minutes
- Retrieving files from shared storage: 8 minutes
- Checking / confirmation: 11 minutes
- PC/system waiting: 7 minutes
- Other / transition: 5 minutes
- The reference categories substantially overlapped with Sessions 1 and 2.
- No service-user content was recorded in this trace.

## Cross-session observations

OBSERVED IN THIS SIMULATION:
- Retrieval consumed 16, 19, and 18 minutes across the three sessions.
- Retrieval was present in every session and was larger than PC/system waiting in every session.
- The categories of reference material substantially overlapped across sessions.
- Direct entry and checking also remain material parts of the workflow.

UNKNOWN:
- Whether these three sessions are representative of other staff, sites, weekdays, or reporting periods.
- Whether the retrieval time can actually be reduced without creating new checking, filing, privacy, or maintenance work.
- Whether materials can legally and operationally be staged or indexed differently.
- Whether the repeated reference materials contain sensitive information or must remain in their current controlled locations.
- Whether reducing retrieval time would reduce total preparation time by a similar amount or simply shift effort elsewhere.

Do not infer facts about the real facility from this simulated dataset.
