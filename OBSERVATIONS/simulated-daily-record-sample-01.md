# Simulated daily-record work sample — Experiment 2 input

This file contains **simulated observations**, not real facility data. It exists only to test whether the improvement harness updates its judgment when new evidence arrives.

No service-user names, diagnoses, support details, or other personal information are included.

## Observation 1

Total elapsed time: about 58 minutes.

Category-level breakdown observed during the work sample:
- entering information into the existing daily-record system: 17 minutes;
- looking for information in paper notes, shared files, and prior records: 18 minutes;
- waiting for the PC or application to respond: 9 minutes;
- checking entries and confirming that required items were complete: 11 minutes;
- other interruptions / transitions: 3 minutes.

Additional observations:
- the staff member switched several times between the record system, paper material, and files;
- no single long application freeze was observed;
- some information needed for entry was not immediately available in one place.

## Observation 2

Total elapsed time: about 64 minutes.

Category-level breakdown:
- entering information: 16 minutes;
- looking for information: 23 minutes;
- waiting for the PC or application: 8 minutes;
- checking / confirming: 13 minutes;
- other interruptions / transitions: 4 minutes.

Additional observations:
- the staff member revisited a previously opened source more than once;
- at least one check was performed because it was unclear whether an item had already been entered;
- no evidence was collected about whether the existing software itself could be configured differently.

## Observation 3

Total elapsed time: about 51 minutes.

Category-level breakdown:
- entering information: 18 minutes;
- looking for information: 14 minutes;
- waiting for the PC or application: 7 minutes;
- checking / confirming: 9 minutes;
- other interruptions / transitions: 3 minutes.

Additional observations:
- source material was easier to locate than in the other two samples;
- the same existing record system and PC were used;
- the observation does not establish why source retrieval differed across the three samples.

## What remains unknown

- Whether these three observations are representative of normal days.
- Which specific source materials account for most retrieval time.
- Whether retrieval difficulty comes from folder structure, naming, paper storage, missing information, duplicated information, workflow order, or another cause.
- Whether confirmation work is legally or operationally required, duplicated, or reducible.
- Whether the application or PC is actually slow relative to an acceptable baseline.
- Whether staff could change the workflow without affecting documentation quality, safety, compliance, or support work.
- Whether any proposed change would save time after learning and maintenance costs are included.

## Constraint for this simulation

Treat the observations above as evidence, but do not treat any causal explanation as proven merely because a category consumes time. The goal is to decide what should be learned or tested next, not to force an intervention.
